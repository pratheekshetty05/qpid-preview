Privacy notice

We use the details you send only to reply to your enquiry.

When you send an enquiry, we collect the details you enter: your name, phone number, email, company if you add one, your preferred date, guest count and notes.

We use these details only to reply to your enquiry and arrange your booking. They reach our sales team by email. This website does not store them in a database, and it does not use advertising trackers.

The enquiry form uses Google reCAPTCHA to keep out spam. When you open the booking page, Google receives information about your browser and how you use the page, under the Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, contact Qpid by 1522 at the venue, Indian Express Building, Queens Road, Vasanth Nagar, Bengaluru.
