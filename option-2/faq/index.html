Questions, answered

Everything people ask about Qpid by 1522 in Bengaluru: where it is, who it is for, what it serves and how bookings work.

About Qpid

What is Qpid by 1522?

Qpid by 1522 is a community bar in Bengaluru built around long shared tables. It is designed to connect different kinds of people, not only couples.

Where is Qpid in Bengaluru?

Qpid is at the Indian Express Building, Queens Road, Vasanth Nagar, Bengaluru.

Is Qpid only for couples?

No. The name is a joke on Cupid. Here, the arrow connects regulars, newcomers, founders, artists, teams and anyone who walks in alone.

Can I come alone?

Yes. Tables are shared, so a solo visit is what the room is built for.

What is the difference between weekdays and weekends?

It is the same room with the same tables. Weekdays run as a community bar. Weekends turn into a full DJ-led night.

Food and drink

What does Qpid serve?

A short drinks list of beer, highballs and one house cocktail, with Indian and pan-Asian bar bites.

Bookings

Do I need a booking for a small group?

Walk in on any weeknight. Tables are shared, so a seat is rarely far away. Send an enquiry for larger groups so we can hold space.

How do I book a group or corporate event?

Fill in the enquiry form on the booking page with your date, guest count and any notes. It goes straight to our sales team.

How soon will sales reply?

Our sales team reads every enquiry and replies using the contact details you share.
