Qpid

[kyoo-pid] noun. Not the love one.

A spirit-forward bar on Queens Road where people with no plan to meet end up in the same round. Comes with properly made drinks, a DJ on weekends and introductions nobody asked for. Known to cause new friends, unlikely business partners, one more round and the phrase “wait, how do you two know each other?”

Synonyms: good company, common ground, the good kind of small talk

Good to know

Quick answers

What is Qpid by 1522?

Qpid by 1522 is a spirit-forward community bar in Bengaluru where all kinds of people come by to have a good time. It is built to connect people, not only couples.

Where is Qpid in Bengaluru?

Qpid is at the Indian Express Building, Queens Road, Vasanth Nagar, Bengaluru.

Is Qpid only for couples?

No. The name is a joke on Cupid. Here, the arrow connects regulars, newcomers, founders, artists, teams and anyone who walks in alone.
