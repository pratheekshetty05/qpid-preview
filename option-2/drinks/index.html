Quick answers

What does Qpid serve?

Spirit-forward cocktails, highballs and beer, with Indian and pan-Asian bar bites.

What does spirit-forward mean?

Drinks built around the spirit itself, so the gin, whisky or rum leads rather than sugar or juice.
