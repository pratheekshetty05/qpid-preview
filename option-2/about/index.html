A bar named after Cupid, for everyone but couples

Qpid by 1522 is a community bar on Queens Road, Bengaluru, built around long shared tables so that different kinds of people end up meeting. It runs as an easy community bar on weekdays and a DJ-led club on weekends.

The idea

Love was too small a job.

Cupid has one trick: an arrow that joins two people. Qpid keeps the arrow and changes the brief. It joins a regular to a newcomer, a founder to a designer, a team to the table beside it.

The tool for that is furniture. Long shared tables mean you sit next to someone you did not come with. On weekdays the room is an easy community bar. On weekends it becomes a DJ-led club. The tables stay.

Good to know

Quick answers

What is Qpid by 1522?

Qpid by 1522 is a community bar in Bengaluru built around long shared tables. It is designed to connect different kinds of people, not only couples.

Where is Qpid in Bengaluru?

Qpid is at the Indian Express Building, Queens Road, Vasanth Nagar, Bengaluru.

Is Qpid only for couples?

No. The name is a joke on Cupid. Here, the arrow connects regulars, newcomers, founders, artists, teams and anyone who walks in alone.

Can I come alone?

Yes. Tables are shared, so a solo visit is what the room is built for.
